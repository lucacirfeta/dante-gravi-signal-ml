# O3a raw-source inventory and acquisition freeze

## Goal

Freeze the public GWOSC O3a 4 kHz HDF5 source inventory and the exact raw
files required by the provisional initial-calibration blocks, without
downloading strain or executing raw acceptance.

## Tasks

1. Query and normalize the public O3a H1/L1 4 kHz HDF5 URL inventory over
   the detector-specific frozen CBC_CAT1 extent.
2. Bind the normalized inventory to the frozen DQ snapshot and its exact
   query semantics.
3. Expand every provisional 17-window block to its complete whitening
   context and map it to the minimum covering set of source frames.
4. Fail closed on URL ambiguity, coverage gaps, detector mismatch, or parent
   evidence drift.
5. Freeze a deterministic acquisition plan for `E:\o3a`; do not download
   bytes, inspect strain, score windows, or execute fallback selection.
6. Verify local regeneration, schema invariants, and Windows/WSL tests.

## Completion gate

The checked-in inventory and acquisition plan regenerate byte-identically
from their frozen inputs, every selected block has complete source-frame
coverage, and both artifacts explicitly report zero downloaded bytes and no
strain or outcome access.
