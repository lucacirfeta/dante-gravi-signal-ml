# O3a morphology taxonomy: decision gate and execution plan

## Current evidence

- O3a CLASSIFY is PASS verified for all 8,900 frozen primary seeds (H1 3,624;
  L1 5,276); its compact receipt is
  `artifacts/dante_light/o3a_native_v1/native_classification.json`.
- The O3a primary-scan SQLite `windows` table retains the candidate MIL
  vectors, detector/GPS identities and image digests needed for an exact join.
- `config/dante_o3a_native_v1_stage_contract.json` freezes initial/native
  calibration, index and class rules, but **does not specify a taxonomy
  representation, linkage, distance cutoff, population or family naming**.
- The verified O4a precedent is
  `config/dante_o4a_corrected_native_taxonomy_v1.json`: all classified primary
  candidates, unchanged primary-scan MIL vectors, L2 normalization, cosine
  distance, single linkage at distance 0.25, historical family naming.
  O4a's largest family contains 10,940/10,942 rows: this is known
  single-linkage chaining, not a physical-coincidence result or evidence of
  distinct glitch types.

## Required decision before source freeze or real execution

Choose whether O3a TAXONOMY must reproduce the O4a method above for strict
methodological parity, including its known chaining limitation. The alternative
is a separately specified morphology experiment (for example different
linkage/cutoff or detector-local clusters); it cannot be presented as the same
O4a/O3a family definition. No taxonomy method is inferred from the general
parity statement alone. Until this decision is explicit, do not create a
production taxonomy contract or execute clustering on real O3a candidates.

## After the decision

1. Freeze a versioned O3a taxonomy contract binding the exact primary-scan and
   classification receipts, runtime, selected method, immutable source hashes
   and 8,900 detector/GPS identities. Preserve historical runs.
2. Adapt the O4a implementation without importing O4a scientific rows.
   Require exact detector/GPS, identity-digest and image-hash joins; finite,
   nonzero MIL vectors; no score/class/population changes; and no prior family
   labels. Taxonomy must not be treated as physical coincidence.
3. Test synthetic partitions, row ordering, missing/corrupt vectors, duplicate
   identities, parent/file/hash mismatches, family naming and deterministic
   replay. Run the O3a/PatchProducer regression suite. Commit the contract and
   adapter on `science/o3-transfer-readiness` before opening real results.
4. Execute TAXONOMY, independently verify every output row and summary against
   the frozen parents, then record family-size distribution and any chaining
   explicitly. Update STATE and summary; commit/push the branch only.
5. Do not open COINCIDENCE or PEM under this authorization. No significance or
   astrophysical-discovery claim follows from TAXONOMY.

## Time estimate

After the method decision: approximately 2–4 hours for contract, adapter,
pre-run tests and source freeze; then approximately 30–90 minutes for execution,
independent replay and post-run checks. Total planning range: 3–6 hours if
parents and runtime remain available. This is an estimate, not an observed run
duration; an infrastructure or provenance failure invalidates it.
