  `artifacts/dante_light/o3a_native_v1/native_classification.json`.

  vectors, detector/GPS identities and image digests needed for an exact join.
- `config/dante_o3a_native_v1_stage_contract.json` freezes initial/native

- The verified O4a precedent is
  `config/dante_o4a_corrected_native_taxonomy_v1.json`: all classified primary

  O4a's largest family contains 10,940/10,942 rows: this is known
  single-linkage chaining, not a physical-coincidence result or evidence of
  distinct glitch types.

## Author decision and pre-registered expectation

On 2026-09-24 the author approved strict O4a-method parity for O3a TAXONOMY:
the same population rule, primary-scan MIL representation, normalization,
cosine distance, single linkage, 0.25 distance cutoff and family naming.
Do not tune the method after opening O3a family outcomes.

**Prediction before execution:** single-linkage chaining is expected to put
nearly all O3a candidates into one dominant family, as on O4a (10,940/10,942).
This is an expectation, not a pass criterion or a target fraction. If O3a
instead yields multiple substantial families, report that discrepancy as an
interesting observation requiring follow-up; do not change the method to make
the runs agree. Either outcome is a morphology-graph result, not physical
coincidence or a global-significance claim.

## After the decision

1. Freeze a versioned O3a taxonomy contract binding the exact primary-scan and
   classification receipts, runtime, approved method, pre-registered
   expectation, immutable source hashes
   and 8,900 detector/GPS identities. Preserve historical runs.
2. Adapt the O4a implementation without importing O4a scientific rows.
   Require exact detector/GPS, identity-digest and image-hash joins; finite,
   nonzero MIL vectors; no score/class/population changes; and no prior family
   labels. Taxonomy must not be treated as physical coincidence.
3. Test synthetic partitions, row ordering, missing/corrupt vectors, duplicate
   identities, parent/file/hash mismatches, family naming and deterministic
   replay. Run the O3a/PatchProducer regression suite. Commit the contract and
   adapter on `science/o3-transfer-readiness` before opening real results.
4. Execute TAXONOMY, independently verify every output row and summary against
   the frozen parents, then record family-size distribution and any chaining
   explicitly. Update STATE and summary; commit/push the branch only.
5. Do not open COINCIDENCE or PEM under this authorization. No significance or
   astrophysical-discovery claim follows from TAXONOMY.
