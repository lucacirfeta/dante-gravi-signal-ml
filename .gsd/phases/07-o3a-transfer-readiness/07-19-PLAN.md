# O3a physical coincidence: method decision before execution

## Verified starting point

O3a TAXONOMY is PASS verified for all 8,900 detector/GPS identities. The
upstream O3a stage contract approves asymmetric ROBUST-seed evaluation with
an opposite-detector partner independent of partner class, and restricts
pooled-null exceedances to a diagnostic PEM shortlist. It does not freeze the
physical statistic, time shifts, pooled-null estimator, quantile, or
multiple-testing policy. The earlier O3a scope contract explicitly listed
`coincidence_null_and_multiple_testing_policy` as unresolved. Thus no O3a
coincidence run or shortlist may be opened solely on the basis of the existing
interpretation rule.

The corrected-O4a precedent is
`config/dante_o4a_corrected_native_coincidence_v1.json`. It measures a
band-passed, light-travel-lag-limited normalized cross-correlation in a
top-K-patch-localized 1 s window, with eight partner shifts of +/-1, 2, 4,
and 8 s. Each measured ROBUST seed contributes its maximum shifted value to
the pooled primary null; the diagnostic threshold is its linear p99. An
on-source value strictly above that threshold is shortlisted. AMBIGUOUS seeds
are measured separately and do not affect the primary threshold; BACKGROUND
is excluded. This procedure is diagnostic and does not account for a global
look-elsewhere effect.

## Decision required

Recommended: approve **exact corrected-O4a measurement and pooled-null
method parity** for O3a, with new O3a-only seeds, strain, index, receipts,
and null values. Carry over numerical measurement settings from the versioned
O4a contract, not from this prose. Freeze those settings and all parent/source
hashes in a new O3a contract before opening outcomes. No O4a scientific rows
or thresholds may be imported. No global-significance claim is permitted.

Alternative: choose a new O3a coincidence statistic, null, or multiple-testing
policy. This is a separate scientific design and cannot be silently implemented
under the existing parity approval. It requires an explicit contract and
pre-run validation plan.

## Execution after decision

1. Bind the exact O3a CLASSIFY, TAXONOMY, INDEX and primary-scan receipts and
   current frozen source hashes. Verify all 8,900 seed identities and class
   counts; do not use taxonomy family labels in physical coincidence.
2. Implement and test O3a adapter with synthetic edge cases, including
   detector/partner locality, exact time and raw-frame mapping, missing/null
   measurements, strict threshold equality, class-pool separation, and
   deterministic replay. Freeze code/contract before real execution.
3. Run and independently verify physical coincidence. Report primary and
   diagnostic populations separately, and label the shortlist diagnostic.
4. Only then prepare a separate O3a PEM contract. PEM channel availability,
   background exclusion, null construction and family-wise procedure require
   their own preflight before any PEM measurement. No A2 promotion.

Until the recommended parity decision or a fully specified alternative is
approved, this plan authorizes only read-only preflight and outcome-blind
implementation preparation, not O3a physical-coincidence or PEM execution.
