ZIP timestamps and permissions, writes entries in stable order, and checks
member uniqueness, safe paths, CRC integrity, size, and SHA-256. Two consecutive
builds produced the same bundle digest shown above.

The package follows the current
[arXiv TeX guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit_tex.html): `main.tex` is
at the archive root, all used figures are included in PDFLaTeX-compatible form,
the required BibTeX database is present, generated auxiliary files are absent,
and the submission-ready date is fixed as `September 8, 2026` rather than
produced by `\today`; the rendered line no longer labels the paper as a draft.

submission.
