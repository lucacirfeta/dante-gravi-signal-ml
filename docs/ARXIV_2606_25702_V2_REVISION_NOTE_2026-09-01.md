## Paper text

### Revision note (v2; 3 September 2026)

A post-publication, identity-preserving replay audit identified a
preprocessing defect in a later provenance-frozen detector-aware O4a catalogue
generated by the published whitening and scoring path. This 10,429-identity
audit catalogue is distinct from the 140-event result population reported in
the historical body of the paper; the two populations are not substituted for
one another in v2. The intended contract whitens each 32-s analysis window
using 4 s of strain context on both sides (40 s in total) before cropping. For
169 of the 10,429 frozen detector--GPS catalogue windows (1.62%), the required
interval crossed an HDF5 file boundary and the historical path supplied only
36 s: 4 s of left context and no right context. The other 10,260 windows
reproduce their published native scores within the frozen absolute tolerance
of 2 x 10^-7, whereas all 169 boundary windows change by more than this
tolerance when the complete symmetric context is restored.

Applying the original detector-specific thresholds and routing rules, with no
retuning, changes 120 of 10,429 detector-specific DSD class assignments
(1.15%) and reverses 97 of 10,429 routing decisions (0.93%); all 97 routing
transitions are from `ESCALATE` to `ROUTINE`. These figures quantify the direct
effect of restoring the missing context while preserving the historical
representation and detector-specific thresholds. They do not estimate the
effect of the subsequent reconstruction, which rebuilt both the
detector-aware reference index and the calibration.

The provenance-locked end-to-end reconstruction is now complete. Relative to
the historical catalogue, it contains 10,022 matched detector--GPS identities,
407 historical-only identities, and 920 corrected-only identities. Among the
10,022 matched identities, 1,626 class assignments differ. This broader churn
must not be attributed solely to the padding defect: 39 changes occur in the
matched historical edge cohort and 1,587 outside it, and every matched score
changes beyond 2 x 10^-7 because the representation as well as the thresholds
was rebuilt. Cross-applying thresholds between those representations would
not provide a valid causal decomposition.

The corrected-only population likewise cannot be labelled as padding-induced
false negatives. Twelve of its 920 members have historical right-edge
geometry, but causality is not established. Two of the nine primary pooled-null
PEM follow-up targets are corrected-only; neither has right-edge geometry and
both receive a `NO_CORRELATION` PEM verdict. The pooled-null shortlist is a
diagnostic follow-up population, not a globally trial-corrected significance
claim. Historical artifacts remain immutable; the corrected workflow enforces
cross-file context stitching and content-addressed provenance.
