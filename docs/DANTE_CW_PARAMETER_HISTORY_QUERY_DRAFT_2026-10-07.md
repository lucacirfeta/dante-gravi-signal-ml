## Evidence and scope

The official [T2500198 v3 table](https://dcc.ligo.org/public/0200/T2500198/003/O4_injection_params.html)
gives an approximate injection epoch stop at GPS1420815618 (13January2025).
The retained public-metadata L1 CW-off January cohort is18..24January2025.
Neither fact proves an amplitude change or a definitive parameter-validity cutoff.
[GWOSC](https://gwosc.org/O4/o4_inj/) describes presence flags, not an amplitude
history. A bounded public search did not establish realized amplitudes and
uncertainty for the proposed CW-on anchoring intervals. Attempts to access
older DCC document-version landing pages through the browser returned errors;
this is not evidence those versions or further documentation do not exist.
O4b release documentation confirms the run extends to28January and points to
the parameter table; no chronology is inferred from a summary plot or general
statements about negligible impact on transient searches.

## Copy-ready query

We are planning a preregistered paired robustness study of an anomaly-detection
pipeline using public O4b L1 strain. Before any decision-score analysis, we
intend to validate a physically modelled software CW injection against real
hardware-injection narrowband power, including actuation/calibration uncertainty.

Could you clarify the following, or point us to the appropriate public record?

1. Does the approximate epoch stop GPS1420815618 in T2500198 v3 only bound the
   tabulated start/stop frequencies, or also parameter/hardware realization?
2. Were Aplus/Across, phases or the set of active injected pulsars changed
   during O4b, particularly around August-September2024 and January2025?
   What documented CW-on intervals are suitable amplitude anchors for each?
3. Is a time-resolved realization/actuation uncertainty record available,
   distinct from nominal source amplitudes and the strain-calibration envelope?
4. Are the binary parameters and nominal phase evolution in that publication
   sufficient for a detector-frame model at the relevant GPS, and are there
   known conventions/corrections needed for the released cleaned strain?

We will not interpret NO_CW flags as realized-amplitude measurements, assume
unchanged amplitudes by extrapolation, or infer detector safety from this study.
