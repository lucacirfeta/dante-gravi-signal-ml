# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive
project environment for contributors, reviewers, and users, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence;
- document uncertainty and distinguish verified results from interpretation;
- welcome reproducibility reports and good-faith correction of errors;
- protect private data, credentials, and embargoed information;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- respect attribution, licensing, and authorship boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate
misrepresentation, publication of private information, and sustained
disruption of technical discussion are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may edit or remove contributions and communications that
violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Conduct concerns should be reported privately through the repository owner's
available GitHub contact channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practicable, with attention to the safety of the reporter and affected people.

This policy applies in project repositories, review discussions, and other
spaces where a person is representing the DANTE project.
